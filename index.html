INSIDE THE PROCESS

Start with the right feedstock.

Pyrolysis is thermal decomposition in an oxygen-free or oxygen-limited environment. The outputs depend on the waste and operating conditions.

01 / ACTION
♻

Select suitable dry feedstock

Wet food waste goes to composting; selected dry residual material can be prepared for thermal treatment.

02 / WHY IT MATTERS
◎

Controlled reactor conditions

Heat decomposes prepared organic feedstock while oxygen is excluded; process gases need appropriate treatment.

03 / CLASSROOM TAKEAWAY
✳

Feedstock segregation

Explain the three main product streams: condensable vapours, non-condensable gas and solid char.
